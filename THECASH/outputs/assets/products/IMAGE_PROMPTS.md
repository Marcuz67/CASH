# Product image prompts

Created with the built-in imagegen tool for the eight sample grocery products. These are generated, unbranded catalog images, not photographs of a specific supplier's actual goods. Images are stored beside this file and served locally by the app. Seller-uploaded photos take priority.

## rice.png

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: square online grocery catalog product photo. Primary request: a 1 kg clear unbranded food-safe bag filled with uncooked white rice, standing upright, with a small scoop of loose rice in front. Style: realistic, ordinary grocery product photography, not an illustration. Full product centered with generous margins; light plain off-white tabletop and background, soft even natural studio light, crisp details, neutral colors. No brand names, no visible text, no logos, no watermark, no people. Image should be an individual product photo, not a grid or collage.
